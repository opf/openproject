# Evaluate Design Options

The conceptual review stage in Feature Evolution.

## Instructions
1. **Generate Alternatives**: Draft at least 2 (two) conceptually distinct viable designs.
2. **Compare Strategies**: Weigh their trade-offs against performance footprint, testing ease, and maintainability.
3. **Make Selection**: Choose the optimal path, providing a documented rationale.
4. **Next Action**: Transition to the `implement-feature-slice` skill.
