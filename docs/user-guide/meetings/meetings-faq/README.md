# Frequently asked questions (FAQ) for meetings

## Can I add a meeting to a calendar?

Yes. You can [download a single meeting as an iCalendar event](../meeting-features/meeting-participants/#download-a-meeting-as-an-icalendar-event), or [subscribe to all your meetings](../#subscribe-to-meetings) in an external calendar so they stay up to date automatically. Participants can also receive [email calendar updates](../meeting-features/meeting-participants/#email-calendar-updates) with an iCalendar invite for a specific meeting.

## Are the meetings shown in calendar widgets?

Yes, the Calendar widget on the [project home page](../../projects/project-home/project-widgets/#calendar-widget) and [My page](../../../getting-started/my-page/#add-widgets) displays meetings. Meeting links in this widget are clickable and open the meeting directly.

## What is the difference between calendar subscriptions and email calendar updates?

Calendar subscriptions and email calendar updates serve different purposes in OpenProject meetings.

**Calendar subscriptions** give you a read-only feed of all your meetings in an external calendar, such as Outlook or Google Calendar, so your meetings stay up to date automatically. You cannot respond to a meeting invitation through a calendar subscription. Learn more about [subscribing to meetings](../#subscribe-to-meetings).

**Email calendar updates** are sent to participants of a specific meeting and include an iCalendar invite. They inform participants about changes to the meeting, such as an updated date, time or location. If your OpenProject instance has [incoming emails](../../../system-admin-guide/emails-and-notifications/#incoming-emails-settings) enabled, you can respond to the invite in your email or calendar client (**Accept**, **Decline** or **Maybe**), and your response is shown as your participation status in OpenProject. Learn more about [email calendar updates](../meeting-features/meeting-participants/#email-calendar-updates).

You can manage general email notification behavior in your [personal notification settings](../../notifications/notification-settings/).

## Can I search the contents of past meetings?

Yes. Since OpenProject 18.0, the contents of meetings, such as agenda items and their notes, can be found using the [global search](../../search/). This makes it easy to look up whether and when a topic was discussed in a meeting.

## Can participants decide which agenda items are discussed first?

Yes. Since OpenProject 18.0, you can switch a meeting to [vote-based sorting](../meeting-features/meeting-agenda/#sort-agenda-items-and-vote). Participants can then upvote or downvote agenda items, and items with the most votes are moved to the top of their section.

## Can I tell participants why a meeting was cancelled?

Yes. Since OpenProject 18.0, you can add an optional reason when [deleting a meeting](../meeting-features/meeting-setup/#delete-a-meeting). When email notifications are enabled, the reason is included in the cancellation email sent to participants.

## What makes OpenProject a great choice for managing meetings?

OpenProject stands out as [a reliable open source solution for meeting management](https://www.openproject.org/collaboration-software-features/meeting-management/), especially for teams that value structure and transparency. Its dedicated meetings module allows users to prepare agendas, document outcomes, and link work packages directly to meetings, making follow-ups more actionable and clear. Because it is part of a broader project and task management platform, everything stays connected and organized. With flexible access controls and the option to choose between a secure SaaS or on-premises setup, OpenProject supports both collaboration and data privacy. It is particularly well suited for teams looking for an open source tool that supports efficient, accountable meetings.
